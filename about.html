Once upon a time

Cofounders Ian O’Meara and Darragh McFeely had a renowned
beachside bistro in New Zealand that served real food. Fresh,
delicious, wholesome, authentic food which was sourced from a local
produce stand called the “The Green Rebel”.

How fitting that name would end up to be. Upon their return to
Canada, their passion and desire for healthy genuine eating increased
and ultimately became contagious under the Green Rebel brand.

The mission is clear

feed people wholesome real ingredients that make the biggest impact
intheir lives. Green Rebel creates the perfect eating environment for
a casual yet efficient experience with a full service attitude.

Not only do we care about what we put in our mouths but we care
about the effect and consequences that food production has on the
environment.

Rebel Bowl program

Let’s reduce packaging waste. The program is simple but effective.
Green Rebel will knock 50 cents off the price of your salad every time
you use a Rebel Bowl instead of a take out container.

Catering FAQ’s

 

How can I place an order?

All orders can be placed via our online ordering system here. Questions can be sent through email at
catering@greenrebel.ca , Our friendly catering manager is also happy to answer any questions over the phone prior to
receiving an order. Please note that we are sometimes unavailable to answer the phone during the mornings when our team
is dedicated to cooking and preparing orders fresh for that day. Please send an email if you cannot reach us by phone.



When is the cut off time?

  • Order for Monday, cut off is 2pm Friday.
  • Order for Tuesday, cut off is 2pm Monday.
  • Order for Wednesday, cut off is 2pm Tuesday.
  • Order for Thursday, cut off is 2pm Wednesday.
  • Order for Friday, cut off is 2pm Thursday.
  • Order for Saturday, cut off is 2pm Thursday.


Do You Accept Last Minute Orders After The 2pm Cut Off Time?

Last minute orders are accepted whenever possible but are subject to availability. These orders must be emailed to
catering@greenrebel.ca, but requests that are sent after business hours will be viewed the following morning. All last- minute
orders are accepted after 2pm the day before will be subject to a 10% surcharge. Additions to confirmed orders after the
2pm cut off time from the day prior are also subject to a 10% surcharge to the items added.



What are the delivery times?

Monday to Friday: 7:00am to 4:00pm,

Saturday: 9:00am to 4:00pm,

Sunday: closed.

Deliveries requested outside of these hours can be discussed on an individual basis.
All deliveries have a 1/2-hour delivery window.



What is the minimum spend?

Monday to Friday: No minimums but HST per delivery applies. Saturday: $1000 + HST per delivery. Deliveries below
minimum spends will unfortunately not be accepted on Saturday.



Do you allow pick-ups?

Lunch orders can be picked up at one of our Green Rebel Stores. No delivery charges apply when picking up.



What are your office hours?

Our office hours are Monday to Friday: 8.00am to 4:00pm,

Saturday & Sunday: Closed.

Our online store is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Orders can be placed anytime and will be reviewed and confirmed during office hours.



Do you cater to all dietary requirements?

We can cater for almost all dietary requirements . When placing your order, please list any dietary needs. If the requirements
are beyond our scope, we will let you know via email. Our food may contain or come into contact with common allergens,
such as dairy, eggs, wheat , soybeans, tree nuts, peanuts, fish, shellfish, or wheat. Please consider this when requesting
special dietary requirements.



How do I make amendments to my order?

For any order amendments, you can edit and update your order via the ordering portal. If help is required, please send your
changes in writing to catering@greenrebel.ca quoting the order number. Our catering manager will facilitate these changes
and provide confirmation when your order has been modified. Please ensure all amendment requests are sent prior to the
order cut off times above.



Delivery Info & Fees

Downtown Business District: $10 delivery
Within 5km of downtown: $25 delivery
Outside 5km of downtown based on distance



Cancellation Policy

Should you need to cancel your order, the following conditions apply:
Cancellations for Tuesday to Friday must be received no later than 4pm the day prior.
Cancellation requests for Monday orders must be received no later than 4pm the Friday prior.
Cancelation for Saturday order must be received by 4pm the Thursday prior.
All cancelations must be sent to catering@greenrebel.ca.

Orders will not be refunded if the cancelation is not received within the timeframe above.



Cancellations received after the cancellation window will not be refunded.

Orders will not be refunded if the cancellation is not received within the timeframe above.



Payment Options

Our system will send a final invoice about 2 hours after your order is delivered on the day of your catering event.

  • You can pay using Visa, Mastercard, or AMEX via the link sent with the invoice.
  • If you are paying with EFT please set that up via your online account used to place the order.
  • If you are paying with EFT please send the transfer upon receipt of the final invoice.
  • If you are paying by e-transfer please send the transfer to elise@greenrebel.ca
